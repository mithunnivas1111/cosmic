Why brands keep switching agencies.

If results looked great in month one and faded after that, one of these four problems is usually why.

Dashboard ROAS, not profit

Platform numbers rarely match your store or bank account. When nobody reconciles ad reports with real orders, refunds and costs, you scale spend on a guess.

Results fade after the early wins

Creative fatigue and audience saturation set in within weeks. Without a weekly testing routine, costs creep up and nobody can explain the drop.

Tracking is quietly broken

Missing purchase events, a half-installed pixel or no Conversions API means your ads learn from partial data and optimise toward the wrong customers.

COD and returns eat the margin

Many Indian brands lose money to cash-on-delivery returns and fake orders, yet never measure them by product, city or campaign.

Questions, answered.

What makes Cosmic different from a generic digital marketing agency?

We work with D2C and e-commerce brands only. We start with tracking and store fixes before scaling ads, and we report on cost per customer, order value and repeat purchase, not only clicks.

Which store platforms do you work with?

Shopify, WooCommerce, Wix, custom-built stores and marketplace-led brands. We care about your data and customer journey more than the software underneath.

What does the free audit include?

A review of your tracking, ad account and store, with a written list of fixes ranked by likely impact. No obligation to hire us.

How fast will I see results?

Tracking and store fixes can show an effect within weeks. Ad performance usually needs four to eight weeks of testing. We don’t promise a specific ROAS.

What ad budget do I need?

The audit has no minimum. For ad management we recommend at least ₹30,000 a month in ad spend so there is enough data to learn from.

How much do you charge?

It depends on your channels, creative needs and ad spend. After the free audit we send a clear, scoped proposal with no hidden charges.

Do you work with brands outside India?

We start with Indian D2C brands and are open to international stores. Calls and reporting run in IST by default, and we can adjust for other time zones.

How will we communicate?

You get a weekly written report and a regular call. Questions in between are answered by message.
