01Why Cosmic

Why brands keep switching agencies.

If results looked great in month one and faded after that, one of these four problems is usually the reason. We build our work around fixing them.

  1. 01

    Dashboard ROAS, not profit

    Platform numbers rarely match your store or your bank account. When nobody reconciles ad reports with real orders, refunds and costs, you end up scaling spend on a guess.

  2. 02

    Results fade after the early wins

    Creative fatigue and audience saturation set in within weeks. Without a weekly testing routine, costs creep up and nobody can explain why performance dropped.

  3. 03

    Tracking is quietly broken

    Missing purchase events, a half-installed pixel or no Conversions API means your ads learn from partial data and optimise toward the wrong customers.

  4. 04

    COD and returns eat the margin

    Many Indian brands lose real money to cash-on-delivery returns and fake orders, yet never measure them by product, city or campaign.

04Approach

How growth compounds.

Most brands run ads in isolation. Growth lasts when acquisition, conversion, tracking and retention are connected.

01 Acquire

  1. Meta and Google ads bring qualified visitors, split by awareness, consideration and purchase intent instead of one mixed campaign.

  2. A store that turns visitors into buyers. We fix trust, speed, offers and checkout before spend scales, because a small lift in conversion multiplies every ad rupee.

  3. Every purchase is recorded properly, so reports show real revenue, cost per customer and returns instead of dashboard guesses.

  4. WhatsApp and email turn first orders into repeat orders, and repeat revenue helps fund the next push on acquisition.

06What we measure

The numbers we manage.

ROAS means little if these four don’t work together. We scale only when they support profit.

CACCustomer acquisition cost
Customer acquisition cost, tied to your margin ceiling and not to competitor benchmarks.
AOVAverage order value
Average order value, lifted with bundles, upsells, free-shipping thresholds and better offers.
LTVLifetime value
Lifetime value, grown through WhatsApp and email retention and sensible repeat-purchase offers.
RTOReturn to origin
Return-to-origin on COD orders, tracked by product, courier and pin code so losses become visible.
